About Me

I study how language is represented across large language models and the human brain—and what their alignment can tell us about intelligence.

I am a PhD student in Artificial Intelligence and Humanities at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Shaonan Wang. I am a member of the LINC Lab—Language, Intelligence, Neuroscience & Cognition.

My work sits at the intersection of large language models, psychology, and linguistics. I explore how representations in large language models align with representations in the human brain, and how this alignment can help explain language processing in both biological and artificial systems.

Research Interests

Representation alignment

Mapping correspondences between the internal representations of language models and neural representations in the human brain.

Language processing

Investigating prediction, semantic perception, and the mechanisms that support language understanding.

Interdisciplinary inquiry

Bringing together computational models, psychology, linguistics, and cognitive neuroscience.
